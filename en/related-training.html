BRAIN GAME CENTER / RESEARCH COLLECTION

Cognitive and perceptual training games

Seven Brain Game Center games and one gaze research framework, covering working memory, cognitive flexibility, visual perception, and auditory perception. Each record includes imagery, mechanics, and primary sources.

The games are documented by their training mechanics. The gaze-contingent framework documents eye tracking that updates the display in real time. The main collection and this topic provide separate data downloads. The framework is also included in the main collection under the same record ID.
