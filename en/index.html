OPEN BY DESIGN

Easy to discover.
Open to build on.

This is an expandable curated collection, not an exhaustive catalog. It includes gaze-based input, gaze-responsive gameplay, and blink mechanics; studies that only measure player attention are excluded.

Suggest a game / report a correction ↗
01

Different interactions, clearly labeled

Primarily gaze-controlled play, gaze-assisted input, and blink interaction are labeled separately. Eye-tracking support does not necessarily mean fully hands-free play.

02

Sources alongside availability

Sources prioritize developers, hardware makers, research teams, and papers. Research prototypes are not necessarily released games. Verification is based on documentation and official pages, not hands-on testing of every title.

03

Open data, transparent rights

Original descriptions and database arrangement are licensed under CC BY 4.0; website code uses MIT. Third-party game images, trademarks, and papers retain their original rights and are excluded. Images are loaded from their source sites and may be affected by those sites’ policies.
